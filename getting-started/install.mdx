To set up Copus, sign in to Copus with your Google account, then sign in to an AI provider and connect Google. You sign in to Copus right after the welcome screen. You do the other two steps on the Home page. Copus runs every task on the AI provider. After you connect Google, Copus learns who you work with and how you write, and starts the work waiting for you in the apps you connect.

- [Install Copus](#install-copus) on your Mac

## Install Copus

Copus runs on a Mac with an Apple chip (M1 or later) and macOS 11 or later.

1. Download `Copus.dmg` from getcopus.ai.
2. Open `Copus.dmg` and drag **Copus** into **Applications**.
3. Open Copus from **Applications**.

Apple has checked and approved Copus, so macOS opens it without a warning. You don't need to install anything else. Once Copus is open, it downloads the programs and models it needs in the background. See [What Copus downloads on first launch](/control/your-data-stays-local#what-copus-downloads-on-first-launch).

To update Copus, download the new `Copus.dmg` and replace Copus in **Applications**.

## Open Copus for the first time

The first time you open Copus, Copus shows a welcome screen. Select **Get started**, then sign in to Copus. Copus then opens the Home page.

If you quit before you finish setup, Copus shows the welcome screen again the next time you open Copus.

## Sign in to Copus

When you sign in to Copus with Google, Copus stores the name and email address on your Google account.

Copus asks you to sign in to Google twice during setup, for two different things:

| Step | What Copus gets |
|---|---|
| **Sign in to Copus** | Your name and email address |
| **Connect Google** | Access to Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Drive |

To sign in:

1. Select **Continue with Google**.
2. In your browser, pick your Google account and approve the request.
3. Go back to Copus.

To sign out, open **Settings** > **Profile** and select **Sign out** under **Copus account**. Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Drive stay connected, and your tasks and files stay on your Mac. To have your Copus account deleted, write to utkarsh@getcopus.ai.

## Sign in to an AI provider

You sign in with a Claude or ChatGPT plan, or with an API key.

1. Select **Sign in** under **Sign in to an AI provider**.
2. Pick a provider:
   - **Claude Code**: Copus runs your tasks on Anthropic's Claude Code agent with your Claude plan. Select **Open the sign-in page**, approve Copus in your browser, paste the code your browser shows into **Paste the code here**, and select **Submit code**.
   - **Codex**: Copus runs your tasks on OpenAI's Codex agent with your ChatGPT plan. Copus opens a browser window. Sign in there, and Copus connects to Codex when you finish.
   - To use an API key from Anthropic, OpenAI, or DeepSeek, select **I'll use my own key**, paste the key, and select **Save key**.

You can add the other providers later and switch between them at any time. See [AI providers](/concepts/ai-providers).

## Connect Google

One Google sign-in connects Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Drive. You connect Google after you sign in to an AI provider.

1. Select **Connect** under **Connect Google**.
2. Sign in to Google in your browser.
3. Approve every permission Google asks for. Copus needs access to all three apps. If you leave any permission unapproved, the connection fails, and Copus asks you to approve everything and try again.

Copus saves your name and email address from your Google account to your profile.

After you connect Google, Copus reads your mailbox, learns about you, and starts its first tasks. The results wait on the Home page for you to review. See [What Copus learns about you](/context/what-copus-learns) and [Work Copus starts before you ask](/working/before-you-ask).

## Skip Google for now

Select **Skip for now** to finish setup without Google.

Copus learns from your mail and starts work on its own only after you connect Google. Until then, you start every task yourself from the message box on the Home page.

To connect Google later, select **Connect Google** on the Home page, or connect each Google app in **Settings** > **Integrations**. See [Integrations](/connections/integrations).

## Change your setup later

- **Your name, email, and about-me note:** open **Settings** > **Profile**. See [Instructions and profile](/context/instructions-and-profile).
- **Your Copus account:** open **Settings** > **Profile** to see the email address you signed in with, or to sign out.
- **Your AI provider:** open **Settings** > **AI Providers** to add a provider or switch providers.
- **Your Google apps:** open **Settings** > **Integrations** to connect or disconnect Gmail, Google Calendar, or Google Drive.
- **Your skills:** Copus starts with the **Essentials** skills switched on. To switch on more, select **Skills** in the sidebar. See [Skills](/working/skills).
