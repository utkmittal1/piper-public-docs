To set up Piper, sign in to an AI provider and then connect Google. You do both on the Home page. Piper runs every task on the AI provider. After you connect Google, Piper learns who you work with and how you write, and starts the work waiting for you in the apps you connect.

This page covers how to:

- [Install Piper](#install-piper) on your Mac
- [Open Piper for the first time](#open-piper-for-the-first-time)
- [Sign in to an AI provider](#sign-in-to-an-ai-provider), with a Claude or ChatGPT plan or an API key
- [Connect Google](#connect-google), and what Piper does once you connect
- [Skip Google for now](#skip-google-for-now), and what you give up
- [Change your setup later](#change-your-setup-later)

## Install Piper

Piper runs on a Mac with an Apple chip (M1 or later) and macOS 11 or later.

1. Download `Piper.dmg` from get-piper.com.
2. Open `Piper.dmg` and drag **Piper** into **Applications**.
3. Open Piper from **Applications**.

Apple has checked and approved Piper, so macOS opens it without a warning. You don't need to install anything else. Once Piper is open, it downloads the programs and models it needs in the background. See [What Piper downloads on first launch](/control/your-data-stays-local#what-piper-downloads-on-first-launch).

To update Piper, download the new `Piper.dmg` and replace Piper in **Applications**.

## Open Piper for the first time

The first time you open Piper, Piper shows a welcome screen. Select **Get started** to open the Home page.

If you quit before you finish setup, Piper shows the welcome screen again the next time you open Piper.

## Sign in to an AI provider

You sign in with a Claude or ChatGPT plan, or with an API key.

1. Select **Sign in** under **Sign in to an AI provider**.
2. Pick a provider:
   - **Claude Code**: Piper runs your tasks on Anthropic's Claude Code agent with your Claude plan. Select **Open the sign-in page**, approve Piper in your browser, paste the code your browser shows into **Paste the code here**, and select **Submit code**.
   - **Codex**: Piper runs your tasks on OpenAI's Codex agent with your ChatGPT plan. Piper opens a browser window. Sign in there, and Piper connects to Codex when you finish.
   - To use an API key from Anthropic, OpenAI, or DeepSeek, select **I'll use my own key**, paste the key, and select **Save key**.

You can add the other providers later and switch between them at any time. See [AI providers](/concepts/ai-providers).

## Connect Google

One Google sign-in connects Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Drive. You connect Google after you sign in to an AI provider.

1. Select **Connect** under **Connect Google**.
2. Sign in to Google in your browser.
3. Approve every permission Google asks for. Piper needs access to all three apps. If you leave any permission unapproved, the connection fails, and Piper asks you to approve everything and try again.

Piper saves your name and email address from your Google account to your profile.

After you connect Google, Piper reads your mailbox, learns about you, and starts its first tasks. The results wait on the Home page for you to review. See [What Piper learns about you](/context/what-piper-learns) and [Work Piper starts before you ask](/working/before-you-ask).

## Skip Google for now

Select **Skip for now** to finish setup without Google. Piper then asks for your name and saves it to your profile.

Piper learns from your mail and starts work on its own only after you connect Google. Until then, you start every task yourself from the message box on the Home page.

To connect Google later, select **Connect Google** on the Home page, or connect each Google app in **Settings** > **Integrations**. See [Integrations](/connections/integrations).

## Change your setup later

- **Your name, email, and about-me note:** open **Settings** > **Profile**. See [Instructions and profile](/context/instructions-and-profile).
- **Your AI provider:** open **Settings** > **AI Providers** to add a provider or switch providers.
- **Your Google apps:** open **Settings** > **Integrations** to connect or disconnect Gmail, Google Calendar, or Google Drive.
- **Your skills:** Piper starts with the **Essentials** skills switched on. To switch on more, select **Skills** in the sidebar. See [Skills](/working/skills).
