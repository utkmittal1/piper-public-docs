After you connect Google, Piper reads your recent mail and puts its first results on the Home page for you to review. You can also start a task of your own at any time by typing in the message box on the Home page.

This page covers how to:

- [Review the work Piper started](#review-the-work-piper-started), and send, refine, or dismiss it
- [Start a task of your own](#start-a-task-of-your-own), from your request to the finished file
- [Find the files Piper made](#find-the-files-piper-made)

## Review the work Piper started

Piper reads your recent threads with the people at the top of your People list. For each thread that needs work from you, Piper starts the work or suggests it. Each task appears on the Home page.

1. Select a task on the Home page to open its result.
2. Read the result. A result can be a document, analysis, or research someone asked you for, a prep document for a meeting, a reply drafted in your style, a follow-up, or a decision with choices.
3. Act on the result:
   - Select **Send** to send a reply, **Create event** to add a proposed event to your calendar, or **Done** when you've finished with a document.
   - Select **Refine** and tell Piper in one line what to change. Piper reworks the same task.
   - Select **Do it** to start a task Piper suggests, or **Decline** to turn the suggestion down.
   - To close a task you don't want, select **Dismiss** and pick a reason. Piper reads your reason in its weekly review.

Piper sends a reply or creates an event only when you select **Send** or **Create event**. [Work Piper starts before you ask](/working/before-you-ask) covers every kind of result and action.

If you skip Google during setup, the Home page lists no tasks from Piper. Start a task of your own from the message box.

## Start a task of your own

<Steps>
  <Step title="Type what you want">
    Type your request in the message box on the Home page. Name the outcome, the audience, the format, and how deep to go. For example: "Summarize the attached board deck into a one-page brief for our VP, in about 300 words, with the three decisions we need."

    When the Home page has no tasks, it shows suggested tasks under the message box. Select a suggestion to start that task.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach your documents">
    Select **Attach files** and pick documents from your Mac. You can also drag files onto the message box, or paste an image.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the provider and model">
    Piper runs the task on your most recent provider and model. To pick a different provider or model, select the control next to **Send**. See [AI providers](/concepts/ai-providers).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the request">
    Press Enter, or select **Send**. Piper opens a conversation for the task and starts work. To start a new paragraph instead, press Shift and Enter together.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer Piper's questions">
    For larger work, Piper asks what would change the result before it starts: the goal, the inputs, the audience, the format, or the depth. Select an answer, type your own into **Something else**, or select **Skip**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the plan">
    Piper posts its steps as a card headed **Execution plan**. A strip above the message box names the step running now.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the finished file">
    Select the file card in the conversation to open the file beside the conversation. Piper opens other file types in your Mac's default app for that type. To open or copy the file elsewhere, select **File actions** on the card and pick **Open in default app**, **Show in Folder**, **Copy Path**, or **Copy Contents**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To ask for changes, reply in the same conversation. Piper keeps your documents, the plan, and your answers. Start a new task for unrelated work. Piper starts a new task with your memory and standing instructions, and the earlier conversation stays in its own task.

To review Piper's plan before Piper acts on it, switch **Planning mode** on in the task's header. Piper writes the plan and waits until you select **Go ahead**.

<Note>
A new task starts in **Manual**, so Piper asks you by default before it sends, posts, or changes anything in a connected app, and before it runs a command that changes something on your Mac. To let Piper do these without asking, switch the task to **Autopilot** in the controls under the message box. [Approvals and permissions](/control/approvals-and-permissions) covers what each mode allows.
</Note>

To return to the Home page from a conversation, select **Home** in the sidebar.

## Find the files Piper made

Piper saves finished work in the task's `final-output/` folder and lists those files under **Artifacts**. Piper saves working material, such as notes, drafts, and data pulls, to `intermediate-files/` and lists it under **Other files**.

To open the task's folder on your Mac, select **Open folder in Finder** on the **Other files** heading. See [Files and deliverables](/working/files-and-deliverables).
