Piper draws in one of two themes: **Moss**, the light theme, and **Dusk**, the dark theme. You can also choose **System**, and Piper follows your Mac's own light or dark setting, using Moss or Dusk to match. Piper starts on **System** after a fresh install.

| Choice | What Piper does |
|---|---|
| **System** | Follows your Mac, and changes with it while Piper is open |
| **Moss** | Stays in the light theme, whatever your Mac is set to |
| **Dusk** | Stays in the dark theme, whatever your Mac is set to |

This page covers how to:

- [Pick a theme](#pick-a-theme)
- [See what the theme changes](#see-what-the-theme-changes), inside a reply as well as around it
- [See what keeps its own colors](#see-what-keeps-its-own-colors), finished files included
- [Pick a theme for a slide deck](#pick-a-theme-for-a-slide-deck), which is a separate choice
- [Collapse the sidebar](#collapse-the-sidebar)

## Pick a theme

Open the user menu, the chip at the foot of the sidebar with your name on it, or **Set up profile** if you haven't filled your profile in. Under **Theme**, select **System**, **Moss**, or **Dusk**. If the sidebar is collapsed, expand it first, because Piper hides the user menu with it.

Piper changes the whole window as soon as you select a theme. Piper applies your theme before it draws the first frame, so Piper opens in your theme when you reopen it. The same menu holds your profile and **AI Providers**.

<Tip>
  Piper stores your theme on the Mac you set it on. On a second Mac, Piper starts on **System**.
</Tip>

## See what the theme changes

Piper draws the charts and diagrams inside a reply from a set of series colors that belongs to the theme. The eight colors in **Moss** are earthy and anchored on its rust accent. The eight in **Dusk** are brighter and more pastel, so they stay legible against a near-black background. Piper redraws a chart when you switch theme. Piper also draws cards, tables, the dashboards it builds into a reply, and the file viewer in your theme.

<Note>
  Piper follows one macOS setting besides light and dark. Turn Reduce Motion on and Piper draws a still frame in place of each of its animations. Piper applies the change at once.
</Note>

## See what keeps its own colors

Piper shows a deck it wrote in the slides' own colors in either theme, because those colors belong to the file.

Piper's preview of a Word document or a spreadsheet uses your theme in place of the file's formatting. The file on disk keeps its own formatting, and you see it when you open the file in Word or Excel. Piper draws the structure of a Word document, its headings and paragraphs and lists, on a page in your theme. Piper draws a spreadsheet as a plain grid in your theme. In both previews, Piper leaves out the fonts and fills of the original.

Piper opens a saved HTML page on its own white canvas, the way a browser shows it. [What Piper hands back](/working/files-and-deliverables) covers the viewer and the file card.

## Pick a theme for a slide deck

A slide deck has a theme of its own, separate from Piper's. Ask Piper for a deck and Piper asks you two questions first: whether you want a web deck or an editable PowerPoint file, and where the deck's look should come from. Answer the second question with a built-in theme and Piper offers a grid of theme cards under the question **Which look?**. Eight themes ship: Piper, Boardroom, Signal, Mist, Meadow, Noir, Harbor, and Orchid. The theme you pick sets the deck's colors and fonts. Piper's own theme stays as it is.

Attach a template of your own, or a screenshot of a deck to match, and Piper skips the theme cards. Piper builds the deck from what you gave it.

Piper draws each theme card from the same file it later reads to build the deck, so the deck looks like the card you selected. [Skills](/working/skills) covers how Piper builds decks.

## Collapse the sidebar

Select the panel button at the top of the sidebar, labeled **Collapse sidebar**. Piper hides the task list, the bell, the settings button, the user menu, the wordmark, the back and forward arrows, and the words on the **New task** button. Piper shows **About you** and **Projects** as icons without their labels. Select the same button, now labeled **Expand sidebar**, to bring it all back. Piper keeps the sidebar the way you left it.

Piper also folds the sidebar to its icons when you drag the file viewer wider than the conversation beside it can spare. Narrow the viewer or close it, and Piper opens the sidebar again. Select **Expand sidebar** while the viewer has it folded, and Piper keeps the sidebar open until you close the viewer or drag it again.

Piper hides the theme switcher and the settings button with the sidebar, so expand the sidebar to reach either. Press Escape to close Settings. If a dialog or a drawer is open on top of Settings, Piper closes the dialog or drawer first.
